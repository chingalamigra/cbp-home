What's Happening in Plain English

📍

Your Location is Always Known

The app continuously tracks where you are, where you've been, and where you go. This isn't for your benefit—it's for surveillance.

🔐

Your Identity is Recorded

Facial recognition and biometric data are being collected and stored. This creates a permanent digital record of who you are.

👥

Your Relationships Are Monitored

Access to your contacts and communication patterns reveals who you know and who you associate with.

🎮

Your Behavior Can Be Controlled

Remote monitoring and control capabilities mean your device can be used to influence your actions and decisions.

Why Should You Care?

🛡️

Your Fundamental Rights

Privacy is a fundamental human right. Surveillance on this scale violates that right and enables potential abuse.

💔

Risk to Vulnerable People

Domestic abuse survivors, refugees, activists, and journalists are at heightened risk from this level of surveillance.

🚨

Potential for Misuse

Today's surveillance tool is tomorrow's tool for political persecution, blackmail, or oppression.
